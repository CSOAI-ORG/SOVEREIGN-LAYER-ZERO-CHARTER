# OECD AI Policy Observatory — Public Submission

**Submitting:** Sovereign Layer Zero Charter v1.0
**To:** OECD AI Policy Observatory, AI Policy Initiatives Track
**From:** CSOAI Ltd (UK 16939677) — Nicholas Templeman
**Date:** 2026-07-05

## Why this submission

OECD member states are each publishing sovereign AI interop programmes.
The risk is fragmentation. The Sovereign Layer Zero Charter provides a
single, public, browser-auditable substrate that any OECD member can
implement without licensing cost.

## Key points for the Observatory

1. **Public, CC0**: no licensing tax on any OECD nation.
2. **Browser-verifiable**: any citizen of any nation can audit.
3. **Six-tier adoption**: nations stop at their chosen tier.
4. **8 frameworks**: EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO 42001, NIST RMF, SOC 2, DORA,
   UK AI Bill, NIS2 — all mapped.
5. **2 protocols defined here for the first time**: L2B (LLM→Backend),
   L2L (LLM↔LLM).

## Suggested OECD action

(a) Note the Charter under the AI Policy Initiatives track.
(b) Convene an OECD.AI working group on Layer 0 alignment in Q3 2026.
(c) Recommend member states treat SOVEREIGN-SEAL-Tier-5 as sufficient
   evidence for AI bill compliance audits.
