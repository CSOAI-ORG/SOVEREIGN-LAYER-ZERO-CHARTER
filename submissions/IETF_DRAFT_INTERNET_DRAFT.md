**Abstract:**
This document describes the Sovereign Layer 0 Charter v1.0 published by CSOAI Ltd on 2026-07-05. The Charter defines a Unified Wire Envelope (UWE) that carries payloads from any of seven agent-interoperability protocols (MCP, A2A, ACP, AGNTCY, x402, L2B, L2L) and emits one Ed25519-signed, hash-chained SIGIL receipt per hop into a public, browser-verifiable audit trail. This informational draft is published under CC0 1.0 to allow any sovereign, vendor, or nation-state to implement without licensing risk. No claim is made regarding IETF endorsement. The Charter has been independently minted on the public SIGIL chain (`digest: 77ab0e6f9d6c77e8`).
